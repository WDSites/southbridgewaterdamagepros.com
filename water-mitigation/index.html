The carpet is soaked and the wall feels damp: what happens next

That is the moment mitigation exists for. Water has left the pipe or appliance, but it has not stopped moving: it wicks up drywall, soaks into pad and sits in the framing. Standing water goes first through emergency water removal, and then the structure itself is dried and monitored until the readings are normal.

Structural Drying

Structural drying is the controlled removal of moisture from framing, subfloors, walls and ceilings. It starts with a moisture map from meters and a thermal imaging camera, then air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach the dry standard. Mitigation covers the first 72 hours, and the drying work has its own page on structural drying and dehumidification.

Drying equipment and what each piece does

Rental stores sell the same basic machines, so people often ask whether they can do the drying themselves. What matters is the plan, not the box.

  • Air movers: High-velocity fans that sweep dry air across wet floors and wall bases. Placement and count follow the wet area, not the room size.
  • LGR dehumidifiers: Low grain refrigerant units that pull moisture from the air in typical household conditions.
  • Desiccant dehumidifiers: Used in cold spaces and very wet jobs where refrigerant units slow down.
  • Moisture meters: Give numbers for drywall, wood and framing so drying is measured, not guessed.
  • Thermal imaging cameras: Show where moisture hides without opening the wall.
  • Injectidry and panel systems: Move dry air into wall cavities and under floors.

Renting the same machines without moisture mapping often leaves wet spots behind. We do not offer rentals. The equipment is part of a monitored job, with the crew placing it, checking it and removing it when readings are met.

Drying goals and the 24 to 48 hour rule

The goal is dry standard: readings on drywall, wood and framing that match unaffected parts of the same building. The EPA's mold guide says to dry water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and that the key to mold control is moisture control. That is why mitigation is an emergency service and not something to schedule for next week.

Typical drying takes about three to five days for clean-water losses in ordinary materials. Plaster, thick hardwood, wet insulation and Category 3 jobs run longer. The crew should tell you the target readings, not just a number of days. If mold has already started, mold remediation follows the S520 standard and drying alone is not enough.

Monitoring days: what each visit covers

Drying is not set and forget. Each day has a purpose.

  1. Day 1: map and set upMeters and thermal imaging define the wet area. Wet pad and unsalvageable materials come out, and equipment goes in.
  2. Day 2: check and adjustReadings are logged. Air movers are repositioned, and wall cavities that are not drying get panel or injection systems.
  3. Day 3 and later: log progressMoisture content drops toward the dry standard. Humidity in the room is recorded.
  4. Final day: confirm and removeReadings match the dry standard, equipment comes out and a written record is left for your insurer. Rebuilding is covered under water damage repair.

Older Southbridge buildings dry differently

Southbridge grew around the American Optical Company, which closed in 1984 after employing more than 6,000 people, and around mills powered by the Quinebaug River. Mill-era buildings remain in town. The town's 2024 draft hazard mitigation plan reports that about 38.5 percent of occupied homes were built before 1939 and about 58.6 percent are multi-family.

In practical terms, thick masonry and old brick hold moisture, plaster dries more slowly than drywall, and one burst pipe on an upper floor can wet several apartments. Tell the person on the line how many floors and units are involved so the crew brings enough equipment. For a nearby example, see the Charlton service area.

Electrical and sewage warning

If water touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 before the restoration line. Cut power only if the panel is dry. Sewage water is Category 3, so keep people and pets out.
