About Southbridge Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 water damage and mold restoration line for Southbridge, Massachusetts and the towns around it.

Call (508) 446-9244

What this site is

Southbridge Water Damage Pros is a call line for property owners in Southbridge, Sturbridge, Dudley, Charlton, Webster and Oxford who have water damage or mold. The business address is 479-511 E Main St in Southbridge, and the phone number is (508) 446-9244.

When you call, you describe what happened and where. A licensed and insured restoration professional is then sent to the property when one is available. Water damage, flood damage and emergency water removal are the core of the work, and mold remediation is the secondary service that often follows a leak.

This site does not offer fire damage restoration, plumbing repair or basement waterproofing. If your problem is one of those, the page on water damage repair explains where restoration ends and other trades begin.

What to expect when you call

The call is built around getting the damage stopped and documented. Here is the general sequence, though every job has its own details.

  • Questions first: you describe the source of the water, how long it has been there and which rooms and materials are wet.
  • Safety guidance: you are told what to shut off and what to leave alone while you wait, such as the main water valve or a wet electrical panel.
  • Dispatch: a licensed and insured professional is sent when one is available. Same-day response is the goal, never a promised arrival time.
  • Scope and price before work: the professional explains what needs to be dried, removed or rebuilt and how the price is set before work starts.
  • Documentation: photos, moisture readings and a written scope help when you speak with your insurer.

What we do not claim

We do not publish reviews, ratings, license numbers or company history on this site, because the professionals who respond can change from job to job. Ask whoever arrives for their company name, their license and insurance details, and whether their crews work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and the S520 standard for mold. A good company answers those questions without hesitation.

Why the work differs in this part of Worcester County

Southbridge sits in the Quinebaug River Basin, and the town's 2024 draft hazard mitigation plan reports that about 6 percent of its land lies in the 100 or 500 year floodplain. Many buildings are old: the same plan counts about 38.5 percent of occupied homes as built before 1939. Older plaster walls, original supply lines and stone or brick foundations all change how water damage is found, dried and repaired.

The towns around Southbridge add their own differences. Charlton has hilltop houses on private wells, Webster has a large lake and a river with a wide floodplain, and Oxford sits below two flood-control dams on the French River. The service area overview explains what each town page covers, and each one is written from that town's own planning documents.

The practical result is a line that asks about the building, not just the water. A burst pipe in a 1920s triple-decker with plaster ceilings gets a different plan from the same pipe in a newer house with drywall, and the person answering will ask questions to tell the two apart.

If you are comparing options, the pages on water mitigation, emergency water removal and mold remediation explain each service in plain terms, including what it does not cover.

Where the line works

The service area follows the Quinebaug River valley and the roads out of Southbridge: Sturbridge to the west, Dudley to the east, Charlton to the north, Webster to the southeast and Oxford to the northeast. Each town has its own page on the service area overview, with its own ZIP codes and local notes.

The homeowner guides on the blog cover common questions such as cost, insurance and mold, written for this part of Worcester County. If you would rather talk than read, the line is open at any hour on the contact page.
