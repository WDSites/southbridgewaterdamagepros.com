How the six services fit together

Most water losses move through the same order. Water is extracted, the structure is dried, damaged materials are cleaned out, and the building is rebuilt. Each stage has its own page here, so you can read the one that matches where you are in the process.

If water is still coming in or standing in the building, start with emergency water removal. If the water is gone but the walls and floors are still wet, water mitigation covers the drying. Once everything is dry, water damage repair covers the rebuild. Not sure which applies? Call (508) 446-9244 and describe what you see.

What this site does not do

Fire and smoke damage restoration, plumbing repair and basement waterproofing are not part of these services. Restoration deals with the damage that water has already done, and waterproofing or drainage work deals with keeping future water out. The right trade for that is a drainage or foundation contractor.

Safety checklist while you wait

Whichever page fits, these steps keep people safe until help arrives.

  • Stop the source: shut the main water valve if a pipe or fixture is leaking.
  • Power: turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable, and call 911 or your utility for any electrical or gas risk.
  • Contaminated water: keep people and pets out of sewage or floodwater.
  • Valuables: lift small items and electronics out of the water and take photos before moving anything else.
