When water is spreading in Webster

Webster has 17,776 residents and 8,453 housing units packed into about 12.5 square miles, and it lies roughly 10 miles east of downtown Southbridge, past Dudley. The line is answered 24/7, around the clock, including holiday weekends when a pipe bursts in an empty rental.

Shut the main valve, lift valuables and keep out of water that came from the ground or a drain. If the water is dirty, water damage cleanup removes the wet debris, ruined materials and contamination, then sanitizes and deodorizes. For lake, river or groundwater, flood damage restoration is the match.

Mill-era buildings and what water does inside them

Samuel Slater founded early textile mills in Webster, and Cranston Print Works took over a mill in 1936. Route 12 is Main St into the center, and mill-era buildings remain in town.

  • Brick and masonry: Old masonry wicks water upward from the floor and holds moisture for days, so drying can take longer than the surface suggests.
  • Wood floors on joists: Water drops through gaps to the floor below, and the damage often shows up in a different unit or room.
  • Basements under old blocks: Stone and brick basements seep, and a leak from above can pool there unseen.
  • Mixed-use buildings: A shop below and apartments above means a pipe failure upstairs is a problem for two or more households.

Ask any company how it will dry masonry and whether its crew works to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage.

Webster Lake and the French River

Webster Lake, officially Lake Chaubunagungamaug, covers about 1,278 acres with eight islands and two marinas. The Webster Lake Dam is a privately owned Significant Hazard dam. The French River forms the western boundary for about 3.5 miles.

The draft January 2025 hazard plan puts about 1,572 acres, or 16.8 percent of town, in the 100-year flood zone. It lists 947 parcels susceptible to a 100-year flood, 123 with structures, and no repetitive-loss structures. Nine dams are in town, four Significant Hazard and all private. A hazard rating describes consequences, not likelihood. Check your parcel on the FEMA flood map and ask your insurer about flood coverage, since standard homeowners policies usually exclude it.

Flood-prone sites named in the plan include Town Hall, the schools, Mill Bridge and the Lower Gore and Sutton Road culverts. If you work or live near those, keep emergency numbers where you can find them in the dark.

Before we arrive

Four things to do in a Webster home or apartment while a crew is arranged.

Valve and fixtures

Close the main valve. In an apartment, ask the landlord or building manager where the shared shutoff is and whether other units are leaking.

Power

Switch off breakers to wet areas only if the panel is dry. For a flooded basement panel, call the utility or 911 first.

Valuables and papers

Lift documents, electronics and keepsakes. Photograph the water height and the damage before you move things.

People and pets

Keep everyone out of lake, river or sewage water, and away from wet floors that may hide holes or sagging ceilings.

Groundwater, sewer and who to ask

Webster's water is 100 percent groundwater from three wellfields at Bigelow Road, Thompson Road and Memorial Beach Drive. The sewer plant on Hill Street, on the French River, was built in 1950 and upgraded between 2009 and 2011. It serves almost all of Webster, central Dudley and an industrial park in Oxford. Water and Sewer are divisions of the DPW at 38 Hill St, and the Building Department is at 350 Main St on the lower level.

Webster has three I-395 exits, Route 16 runs east toward Douglas and Route 193 runs south from East Village near the lake. ZIP 01570 covers all of these, and the top calls are water damage cleanup and flood damage restoration. Neighbors in Dudley and Oxford are served from the same line.
